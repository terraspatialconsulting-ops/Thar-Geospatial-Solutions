About Us

Thar Geospatial Solutions is a GIS and Remote Sensing consultancy based in Rajasthan, India. We provide professional services in spatial analysis, wildlife conservation, habitat modelling, cartography, GPS surveys, environmental consulting and geospatial research. Our mission is to transform spatial data into actionable insights for research, conservation and sustainable development.
