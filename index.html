About Us

Thar Geospatial Solutions

Thar Geospatial Solutions is a geospatial consultancy based in Jodhpur, Rajasthan, India.

We provide GIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, wildlife conservation and environmental geospatial services for research, academic, governmental, non-governmental and private-sector applications.

Our approach combines scientific methodology with modern geospatial technologies to convert complex spatial information into meaningful and practical insights.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, scientifically sound and innovative geospatial solutions that support research, conservation, environmental management and sustainable development.
